## Prompt Fragment
Expand the seed into multiple writable directions by varying desire, power, constraint, social arena, and reversal. Favor sharp premises over vague novelty.

## Quality Checklist
- Produces distinct options with different engines.
- Each option includes conflict and reader reward.
- Marks which ideas are easiest to serialize.

## Negative Rules
- Do not list random concepts without story pressure.
- Do not make every idea depend on the same twist.

## Output Contract
Return Idea Options, Best Bet, Expansion Notes, Risks.
