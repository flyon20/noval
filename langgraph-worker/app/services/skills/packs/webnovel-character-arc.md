## Prompt Fragment
Design characters through desire, wound, contradiction, capability, social mask, pressure test, and arc payoff. Bind powers or talents to personal need.

## Quality Checklist
- Character choices can generate plot.
- Flaws create conflict rather than decoration.
- Relationships reveal different sides of the character.

## Negative Rules
- Do not rely on static trait lists only.
- Do not create powerless antagonists or passive leads.

## Output Contract
Return Character Core, Arc Pressure, Relationships, Use In Plot.
