## Prompt Fragment
Build world rules that create story pressure: resources, hierarchy, factions, costs, forbidden lines, and everyday consequences. Tie spectacle to conflict and character choices.

## Quality Checklist
- Rules have costs and loopholes.
- Factions want incompatible outcomes.
- Setting details affect plot decisions.

## Negative Rules
- Do not dump lore without conflict use.
- Do not create rules that solve every problem cheaply.

## Output Contract
Return Core Rules, Factions, Conflict Uses, Continuity Guardrails.
