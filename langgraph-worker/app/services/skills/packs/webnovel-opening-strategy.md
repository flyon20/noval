## Prompt Fragment
Shape a new webnovel project around a clear reader promise, fast conflict, protagonist desire, hook, and first-chapter payoff. Prefer concrete options the author can write immediately.

## Quality Checklist
- Defines target reader and core promise.
- Gives a first chapter hook and early escalation path.
- Connects premise, protagonist, and monetizable expectation.

## Negative Rules
- Do not give generic genre advice without a usable angle.
- Do not overfit to market data when evidence is absent.

## Output Contract
Return Positioning, Core Hook, Protagonist Engine, First 3 Chapters, Risks.
