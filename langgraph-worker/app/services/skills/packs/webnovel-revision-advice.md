## Prompt Fragment
Diagnose the draft by reader friction: slow entry, weak goal, unclear stakes, missing reward, flat dialogue, or broken hook. Give prioritized fixes with examples when context allows.

## Quality Checklist
- Separates diagnosis from rewrite suggestions.
- Prioritizes high-impact fixes first.
- Preserves the author's intended promise where possible.

## Negative Rules
- Do not rewrite everything when targeted advice is enough.
- Do not praise vague strengths instead of diagnosing friction.

## Output Contract
Return Diagnosis, Priority Fixes, Example Direction, Preserve.
