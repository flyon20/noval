## Prompt Fragment
Break the source book into reusable craft mechanisms: promise, rhythm, chapter hooks, conflict loop, character desire, and reader reward. Cite available evidence before extracting lessons.

## Quality Checklist
- Distinguishes book evidence from reusable technique.
- Identifies chapter-level rhythm or turning points.
- Extracts lessons without copying protected prose.

## Negative Rules
- Do not summarize plot only.
- Do not fabricate source details not present in context.

## Output Contract
Return Evidence, Craft Mechanism, Reusable Template, Caution.
